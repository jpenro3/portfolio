Why a recording is missing, and what it costs the patient

The chip on a stranded session does not name the error. It names what has to happen next, because that is the only thing the person reading the queue can act on.

Chip What actually happened What it costs Who can fix it
capture The recorder produced no usable video at all. Usually a device or codec problem. CAPTURE_EMPTY Re-record The patient, from the top. The only expensive one.
stalled The recording existed. The transfer made no progress for thirty seconds and died. UPLOAD_STALLED Resend Staff. The patient reopens the link and it retries.
upload The recording existed. The transfer failed after its retries were exhausted. UPLOAD_FAILED Resend Staff. Same as above.
failed Something went wrong that does not fit the three above. UNKNOWN Investigate Us. A row here is a gap in this table.
no chip The tab closed mid-transfer. Nothing reports, because every terminal branch needs a tab that is still open. UPLOAD_ABANDONED · not yet emitted Unknownopen Nobody, until the page can speak on its way out.
The last row is the honest one. Four of the five states are now attributable. The fifth is the case where the patient handed the device back and walked away, and the only fix is a beacon fired on the way out of the page.

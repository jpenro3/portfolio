The through-line

Clarity is the whole job

My design philosophy is clarity and function: users should navigate and interact with data without effort. I turn complex datasets into actionable insight, using charts, graphs, and custom dashboards to make a trend obvious in a glance. The work is not prettier dashboards. It is clearer, more usable, more scalable ones.

Invented form

The Leapfrog Chart

A Fortune 10 automotive client needed dealerships to compete with each other. No existing chart type showed movement the way the story needed, so we made one. It became the company's strategic differentiator, and it is the clearest proof of the instinct: when the existing form lies, build a truer one.

  • 200+ interactive dashboards
  • First full-time hire at Playfair Data
  • Grew from 3 to about 20 people
  • 2023 Inc. 5000

Range

One system, many analytical needs

A collection of dashboards across very different problems, each a distinct approach to the same goal: clear, intuitive, actionable presentation for people who have to decide something.

Data for good

Coral Resilience Data Platform

A platform to collect, analyze, and visualize coral reef health and resilience. A global interactive map of community-shared coral datasets, with environmental and biological analysis, phylogenetic trees, and heat maps that help researchers, conservationists, and policymakers decide where to act.

  • Global interactive coral map
  • Environmental & biological analysis
  • Community-shared datasets
  • Built with AWS Amplify, React, Python
